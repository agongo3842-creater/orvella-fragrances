Before you order

A little
clarity.

Useful information for your first ORVELLA order.

Are all the perfumes currently available?

The collection includes listed stock and four new additions. Asad Elixir, Qahwa, Musamam and Amethyst require availability confirmation. The quantities shown for our existing collection are not live inventory. Please confirm directly with ORVELLA before payment.

Is delivery included in the price?

No. The displayed price is for the perfume. Delivery is charged separately and confirmed using your location before you pay.

Does adding a bottle to my bag reserve it?

No. A bottle is only reserved after ORVELLA confirms the order directly. Adding an item to your bag or saved collection does not hold stock.

Do I pay on the website?

No payment is collected here. Your bag creates a WhatsApp order enquiry. Availability, the final total and official payment details are confirmed directly by ORVELLA.

How do I avoid paying the wrong account?

Only use payment details sent through the official ORVELLA contact after your order has been confirmed. The WhatsApp number shown on this website is +233 53 414 2445.

What are the return and exchange conditions?

Please ask ORVELLA to confirm the return and exchange conditions before you place an order.
